# ZERO IS YOU v9 credits and source policy

## Mathematics

The v9 problem bank contains original game-specific puzzles and wording. Its concept taxonomy and progression are informed by openly licensed mathematics resources rather than copied competition archives.

- **IM® K–12 Math, first edition (2019–2021)**, authored by Illustrative Mathematics®, licensed **CC BY 4.0**. Attribution: “Based in part on IM® K–12 Math authored by Illustrative Mathematics and licensed under CC BY 4.0.” https://illustrativemathematics.org/terms-of-use/
- **OpenMathInstruct-2**, NVIDIA, licensed **CC BY 4.0**. v9 uses its existence and diversity taxonomy as a reference for maintaining a broad problem-bank architecture; the 12+ GB dataset is not bundled into the game. https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/OpenMathInstruct-2

The labels **50, 75, 90, 95, 99, 99.9** are internal game challenge bands. They are not normed scores, measured percentiles, IQ estimates, or claims about a child’s population percentile.

## Music

ZERO v9 does not redistribute recorded music or downloaded MIDI files. Music is synthesized in-browser with WebAudio from symbolic note sequences.

- Original tracker-style space tracks were written for ZERO v9.
- Classical tracks are new chiptune arrangements of public-domain compositions by J. S. Bach, W. A. Mozart, and L. van Beethoven.
- The **Mutopia Project** was used as a reference for openly reusable classical score availability and licensing practices: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/
- **BeepBox** (MIT) was evaluated as an open-source browser-synth option. v9 ultimately uses a smaller purpose-built WebAudio tracker to avoid adding a runtime dependency: https://github.com/johnnesky/beepbox

## Art

All active gameplay graphics are drawn procedurally in JavaScript/CSS/SVG at a native **32×32 logical-pixel tile density**. No generated-image assets are used.
